## Product

**TicketPulse** — Zimbabwe's premier event ticketing and management platform. EcoCash-first payments, instant ticket delivery, no account required to buy.

## Register

product

## Users

Four distinct user types, each with their own dashboard:

1. **Admin** — TicketPulse operators. Monitors platform health, revenue, active events, pending reviews (draft events, unverified users), Velocity payment reconciliation. Power users who need dense information and fast action buttons. Uses at a desk, multiple times per day.

2. **Organizer** — Event creators selling tickets. Needs to know: how are my events selling, when do I get paid, who are my top buyers. Creates events, manages tiers, exports attendees, runs the scanner at the gate. Mobile-aware — may check from a phone backstage at an event.

3. **Attendee** — Ticket buyers. Primarily sees their purchase history, upcoming events with QR tickets, order status. Simple, reassuring, fast to navigate.

4. **Vendor** — Caterers, photographers, sound engineers, security, etc. who list their packages on the marketplace. Dashboard is primarily a profile editor — keeping their listing sharp so organizers book them.

## Brand

- **Tone:** Confident, direct, no filler. Never corporate-bland.
- **Feel:** Premium but grounded. Not fintech-sterile. Not startup-playful.
- **Zimbabwe context:** EcoCash is dominant payment rail. Events are community and celebration.

## Anti-references

- Generic "SaaS dashboard" — navy sidebars, white cards, everywhere-same-padding
- The hero-metric template — big number, small label, gradient accent repeated in a grid
- Dark mode "just because tools look dark"
- Gradient text
- Side-stripe card borders

## Strategic principles

1. **Action over information.** What needs doing right now is always more prominent than historical data.
2. **Role clarity.** Each dashboard should be unmistakably for its user. Admin sees platform breadth; organizer sees their slice; attendee sees their tickets.
3. **Mobile-aware.** Organizers check on phones at events. Nothing should require horizontal scroll on mobile.
4. **Density with air.** Zimbabwe market — users on mid-tier Android phones, smaller screens, real work to do. Information-dense but not cramped.
